# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Product Manager (primary). They are in a product development situation, seeking to understand user behavior and improve product flows. Their job is to get actionable insights for product decisions.

## Product Purpose
UseWorldview enables product teams to deploy AI personas that explore their product, interact with it, and report back evidence-backed insights in minutes, making user research fast, affordable, and deep.

## Positioning
Unlike traditional user research or basic AI tools that only scan homepages, UseWorldview creates full psychographic AI personas that navigate the entire product and provide traceable, evidence-based recommendations.

## Operating Context
Used by product teams during development cycles to test features, flows, and designs before launch. Accessed via web app, no installation required.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Capabilities: AI persona generation, whole-app exploration, evidence-based insight generation, fast turnaround.
Constraints: Must maintain AI-powered synthetic user research core, preserve brand aesthetic, keep Next.js/Tailwind stack, maintain focus on actionable evidence-based output.

## Brand Commitments
Name: UseWorldview. Visual identity: premium, light-first aesthetic with violet accents (as seen in globals.css). Voice: professional, clear, helpful.

## Evidence on Hand
Functioning Next.js app with designed UI (evident in frontend/app directory). The UI reflects the brand's visual identity and design system.

## Product Principles
1. Depth over speed: Provide psychographically rich AI personas, not just demographic tags.
2. Whole-product exploration: AI agents navigate the entire app, not just landing pages.
3. Evidence-based insights: Every recommendation is tied to specific user actions.
4. Accessibility: Designed for product teams needing fast, reliable research.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
The product aims to be accessible to users with disabilities, following WCAG principles. Evidence: focus outlines, semantic HTML, and color contrast considerations in the CSS.
